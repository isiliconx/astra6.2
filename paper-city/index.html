THE WORKING PLATE

Paper, under constraint.

Each house is a pair of rigid panels. The two feet stay glued to opposite pages, so the ridge has only one place to go as the book opens. The striped canopies are separate little hinges.

MAKER’S INSERT   /   1:1 SCALE

Make the mechanism.

Three A4 sheets with the base, four houses, canopy pieces and a hand-operated tram token.

Read the mechanism & validation notes

The two roof lengths and the spread between the feet form a moving triangle. The ridge is the outer intersection of two circles centered at the feet. Closing the book stacks each house along its original flat crease. The canopy motion is illustrative: fold these tabs by hand in the printed version.

Tests sweep the book through 0–180°, checking panel lengths, page clearance of the main panels, closed alignment, separate house footprints and a reserved tram corridor. This is a zero-thickness model, not a physical build test or a tolerance study. Remove the tram and fold canopies flush before closing.
