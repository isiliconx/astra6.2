An experiment in point of view

The Wrong Camera.

A glove goes missing on a railway platform. Two editors cut the same twenty-five seconds. One sees a thief. One sees a kindness. Neither actor takes a different step.

One stage. One clock.

Switch cuts mid-shot: time, feet and glove stay put. The two edits change camera position, lens, and the moment of the cut. The witness camera never moves. Scrub frame by frame at pickup and handoff.

The scene is silent on purpose. The two readings are editorial intentions, not the finding of an audience study.
