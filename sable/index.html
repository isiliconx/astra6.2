How to play

WASD to move. Drag the view or use the left and right arrow keys to look. Stand near a mirror and tap E or Q to turn it one notch. Power every receiver, then walk through the gate.

On a phone, tap a mirror on the survey map to walk to it, then tap it again to rotate. All three chambers can be completed from the map.

Projection room

Live renderer: Starting optics. The full 3D room traces light rays against mathematical surfaces. WebGL2 is the second renderer; tactical mode preserves the full puzzle if neither 3D backend is available.

The gate relief is a Blender asset. The still above uses the editable studio scene; the moving view is rendered in your browser.
