    03 / Instrument notes

    What counts as a discovery?

    These are simulated, normalized light curves with a trapezoidal transit and independent Gaussian noise. The two candidate periods, depth, duration and baseline are known. This is a test of observation design, not a real exoplanet discovery.

    Orbit odds come from the likelihood of every exposure, starting at 50:50. Your Brier loss is the squared difference between your stated probability and the event, where lower is better. The sealed last window never updates the model: it tests what you learned.
